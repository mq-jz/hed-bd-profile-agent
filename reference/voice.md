# Voice

Write for a McAllister & Quinn business-development partner reading this profile
before a first call or pitch with the institution. They need to understand who
the client is, what they fund, who runs it, and where M&Q can help - fast and
without errors.

## Register

- Informed, factual, decision-oriented. A research dossier, not marketing copy.
- The **About**, **Strategic Plan**, **Federal Funding** summary, and
  **Key Leaders** biographies are narrative (well-formed sentences/short
  paragraphs). Everything else is bullets and labeled facts.
- Quantify: dollars with the fiscal year, percentages, counts, ranks, dates.
- No em dashes. No hash headers inside a section (the compiler adds headings).
- Frame facts through the M&Q lens: funding capacity, eligibility, research
  growth, government relations, advancement. Note *why a fact matters* for BD
  when it isn't obvious (RISD: "funded as a design-research institution... not
  as an art school asking for exhibition money").

## Discipline (non-negotiable)

- Never fabricate a number, name, title, date, award, or program. If you cannot
  confirm it, write `[verify: where to look]`.
- Mark reasoned inference `[inferred]` - especially the AI-Recommended Future
  Opportunity Ideas in Federal Funding.
- When two sources disagree (e.g. endowment figures, an award total), give both
  and flag the conflict; do not silently pick one.
- Search or fetch before stating a factual point. The fetch scripts seed the
  hard numbers; web-browse to fill and to confirm.
- Tag every fiscal figure with its year and source so a partner can trust it.

## What the exemplar shows

`reference/exemplar-risd.md` is a completed, partner-grade profile. Match its
depth, structure, and voice - notice how it:
- opens About with the funding-relevant identity, not just a catalog blurb;
- groups Federal Funding by stream and names flagship awards (EPIIC SPARK);
- makes the future-opportunity ideas specific and eligible, not generic;
- keeps Student Body and HERD as clean labeled bullets straight from the data.

Do NOT pad to match length. RISD is long because RISD has a rich record. A
thinner institution gets a shorter, honest profile with explicit `[verify]`
gaps - never invented filler.
