===== SECTION: Grants Office =====
McDaniel College has no grants, sponsored programs, or research administration office. The college's published offices directory lists 38 offices and none covers sponsored programs, research administration, or grant compliance, and the official org chart shows no such unit under any of the four vice presidents.

Grants are handled by Institutional Advancement, led by Vice President David Sears, and specifically by the Development unit's Christina Goodman, Executive Director of Corporate and Foundation Relations, whose remit is corporate and private foundation solicitation.
