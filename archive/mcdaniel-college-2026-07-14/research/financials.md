===== SECTION: Foundation Funding =====

Private foundation money at McDaniel follows health workforce, not research. The standout is the Kahlert Foundation's $2,500,000 grant (October 2024) to the college's new Bachelor of Science in Nursing program, which followed an earlier $150,000 Kahlert grant for program development. McDaniel named its nursing education center for the Kahlert family in May 2026, and the B.S.N. launches in fall 2026 alongside a clinical partnership with LifeBridge Health at Carroll Hospital. The Sherman Fairchild Foundation's four-year, $580,100 grant (announced April 2026) for laboratory and teaching equipment in Biology, Chemistry, and Health Sciences reinforces the same build-out. Separately, McDaniel endowed a $2,000,000 professorship in Computational Innovation and Emerging Technologies through the state's Maryland E-Nnovation Initiative Fund (August 2025), a program that required privately raised matching dollars, which is further evidence of working advancement capacity.

National foundation exposure is thin and dated by contrast: the Mellon Foundation gave $48,000 in 2003 and $50,000 in 2001 (the latter awarded under the former name, Western Maryland College), both for curriculum and faculty development, and nothing comparable has followed. For M&Q, the read is that McDaniel converts regional health-system and family-foundation relationships well but has no current national foundation portfolio to build on, so the near-term play is packaging the nursing and health-sciences momentum for national health-workforce funders rather than pitching a research story.
