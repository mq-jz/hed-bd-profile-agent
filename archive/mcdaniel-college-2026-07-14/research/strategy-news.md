===== SECTION: Strategic Plan =====
McDaniel is executing "Reaching New Heights: McDaniel College Strategic Plan 2022-2027," endorsed by the Board of Trustees on October 12, 2022 and led by President Julia Jasken. It is organized around four initiatives (Student Success, Employee Satisfaction, Community Relations, Business Operations) and was built over a year by a 30-member steering committee drawing on surveys, small-group discussions, and open forums. Link: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/ReachingNewHeights

For M&Q the plan's usable content is concentrated in Initiative 4, Business Operations, which is explicitly a revenue plan. Goal 4.1 is to grow Graduate and Professional Studies enrollment and revenue through new graduate programs and bachelor's-to-master's pathways with external partner institutions. Goal 4.2 commits to "Launch a comprehensive campaign to fund strategic plan priorities" and a multi-year budgeting model. Goal 4.4 is the direct opening: it names "Increase grant funding and support for faculty research" and "Develop strategies to holistically involve corporations and foundations in the college" as institutional action items. McDaniel has therefore written grant growth into board-approved strategy, which is the cleanest possible entry point for a pitch. Link: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/ReachingNewHeights/BusinessOperations

The read a partner should take from this: grants are positioned as an advancement and advocacy line, not as a sponsored-programs or research-office build. The plan contains no goal to stand up research infrastructure, no research expenditure target, and no government-relations goal. McDaniel wants more external money without having built the internal apparatus to chase it, which is where an outside firm fits rather than competes.

The plan's growth engine is health professions and education, and those are the parts of McDaniel with real federal and state funding streams behind them. The plan named nursing, occupational therapy, and speech language pathology as new programs to introduce; all three now exist or are in accreditation (see Recent News), and each has already pulled non-tuition money: $830,000 in FY26 congressionally directed spending for the nursing facility and $855,646 in Maryland Grow Your Own Educators funding for a special education pathway. The pattern to pitch against is federal and state workforce money for clinical and teacher-preparation programs, not basic research.

Advancement capacity is proven, which matters for whether McDaniel can pay for and cost-share on a program. The plan's Goal 4.2 campaign is live as "Reaching New Heights: The Campaign for McDaniel College," a $50 million comprehensive campaign that had raised $48.8 million as of December 15, 2025 and runs through May 31, 2028. FY2025 (June 1, 2024 to May 31, 2025) was the largest single fundraising year in college history at more than $23 million. Campaign priorities include student-faculty summer research grants and an endowed professorship in Computational Innovation and Emerging Technologies. Link: https://campaign.mcdaniel.edu/about-the-campaign/

The plan expires in 2027 and McDaniel has published no successor plan or successor planning process. [verify: watch the President's Office Initiatives and Plans page, https://www.mcdaniel.edu/about-us/presidents-office/initiatives-and-plans, for a 2027+ plan; a plan refresh would be a natural pitch window]

===== SECTION: Mission Statement =====
"McDaniel College is a diverse, student-centered community committed to excellence in the liberal arts and sciences and professional studies. With careful mentoring and attention to the individual, McDaniel changes lives. We challenge students to develop their unique potentials with reason, imagination, and human concern. Through flexible academic programs, collaborative and experiential learning, and global engagement, McDaniel prepares students for successful lives of leadership, service, and social responsibility. We strive to place students at the center of a humane environment so that they may see and work toward their personal goals while respecting others and sharing the responsibility for the common good."

Source: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/about-us/mission-and-history

The strategic plan carries a shorter articulation: McDaniel College changes lives by inspiring students to achieve educational excellence and realize their potential within a supportive, inclusive, and diverse community.

===== SECTION: Centers and Institutes =====
McDaniel operates no research centers or institutes. The named centers are teaching and student-experience units, which is consistent with a teaching-focused master's college.

- McDaniel Environmental Center: a 58-acre property in New Windsor (30 acres forest, 20 acres fields, 8 acres student farmland) on the former Singleton-Mathews Farm, used as an outdoor learning lab for agriculture, conservation, and environmental sciences. The only center with a plausible sponsored-project surface (USDA, NRCS, Maryland environmental and agricultural programs). Link: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/academics/environmental-studies/mcdaniel-environmental-center
- Center for Experience and Opportunity: career and experiential-learning hub connecting students to internships, fellowships, jobs, study abroad, and alumni mentors. Link: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/academics/center-experience-opportunity

Related named academic programs, not centers: the Lumen Scholars Honors Program, Global Fellows, National Security Fellows, the Encompass Distinction in innovation and entrepreneurship, and McDaniel Budapest (semester abroad in Hungary). Link: https://www.mcdaniel.edu/academics/special-academic-opportunities

Evidence McDaniel can win and administer state workforce money through its education school. https://www.mcdaniel.edu/news/mcdaniel-and-frederick-county-public-schools-partner-create-new-pathway-special-education

- McDaniel College names experienced academic administrator as new provost (June 5, 2026): Caroline R. Sherman, Ph.D., previously vice provost for academic administration and dean of graduate studies at The Catholic University of America, became provost and chief academic officer effective July 1, 2026. A brand-new academic lead whose prior job was running graduate studies, which lines up with the plan's graduate growth goal. https://www.mcdaniel.edu/news/mcdaniel-college-names-experienced-academic-administrator-new-provost

- Largest gift in college history ushers in public launch of $50 million comprehensive campaign (October 10, 2025): An approximately $15 million bequest from Jonas Eshelman '50 anchored the public launch of "Reaching New Heights: The Campaign for McDaniel College," which had over $40 million in gifts and pledges at launch.
