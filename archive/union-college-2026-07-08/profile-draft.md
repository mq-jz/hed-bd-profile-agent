## About

Union College is a private, nonprofit residential liberal arts college in Schenectady, New York. Chartered in 1795, it was the first college chartered by the Board of Regents of the State of New York and is credited with laying out the first planned college campus in the United States. Union enrolls roughly 2,046 students, almost entirely undergraduates, across 21 academic departments with a 9 to 1 student-faculty ratio. Its defining feature is the integration of the liberal arts with engineering, taught here since 1845; from a funding standpoint Union is a research-active baccalaureate college with a modest, NSF-concentrated federal footprint, not a research university.
Federally, Union has drawn about $15.9 million across 57 awards since 2016, led by the National Science Foundation (about $8.98 million), with one-time Department of Education pandemic relief; excluding that relief, competitive research runs closer to $10 million per decade.
On the private side, publicly documented foundation support since 2013 totals at least $36.6 million, dominated by alumni family foundations and led by the Templeton family's foundation-routed payments against $91 million in announced commitments (see Foundation Funding).

## Foundation Funding

**Foundation Funding Total 2013-2025: $36,640,257 (22 awards)** [verify: publicly documented grants only, reconstructed from funder 990-PF filings and announcements; the Candid / Foundation Directory export will be higher - pull it for the authoritative total and companion spreadsheet]

Foundation money at Union runs overwhelmingly through alumni family foundations rather than national competitive grantmakers, and the amounts are transformational. The Richard and Mary Templeton Foundation (Rich Templeton '80, longtime Texas Instruments CEO) paid Union $9.04 million in program support in its FY2024 990-PF alone, installments against the couple's announced $51 million (2020, largest gift in college history) and $40 million (2024) commitments to build the Templeton Institute for Engineering and Computer Science. The Schuler Education Foundation selected Union as one of its first five partner colleges and completed a $22 million match in January 2022 against $20 million Union raised, expanding Pell-eligible enrollment; regional funders such as the Fred L. Emerson Foundation gave into that match. The same named-building loyalty shows in the Feigenbaum Foundation (recurring every year since 2014) and the Karp Family Foundation. National program funders are present but thinner: Mellon has given steadily but modestly since 2013, and HHMI awarded $505,000 in 2022 under Inclusive Excellence 3. The M&Q read: the foundation pipeline is alumni-wealth driven and already well-cultivated, so the whitespace is competitive institutional grantmakers (Teagle, AVDF, Sherman Fairchild science programs), plus leveraging the Templeton engineering buildout and Schuler access cohort as match and program hooks for foundation and federal proposals.
