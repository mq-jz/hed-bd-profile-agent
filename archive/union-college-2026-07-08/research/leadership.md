Elizabeth Kiss became Union's 20th president on July 1, 2025, the first woman to lead the college in its 230-year history. She previously served as chief executive of the Rhodes Trust at the University of Oxford and as president of Agnes Scott College, and she succeeded David R. Harris. Her arrival makes a successor to the lapsed 2020-2025 strategic plan likely, a natural moment to align federal and foundation strategy with new priorities.
