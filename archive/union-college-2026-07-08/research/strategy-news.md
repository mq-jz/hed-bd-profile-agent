===== SECTION: Strategic Plan =====
Union's most recent published strategic plan is "The Power of Union: A Vision and a Plan" (2020-2025), released in October 2019. Through the M&Q lens it centers on Union's differentiator, integrating the liberal arts and engineering, and on immersive, experiential learning; those priorities map to sponsored-research growth, STEM capacity, and advancement. The plan's five-year horizon has lapsed and a successor under President Elizabeth Kiss is expected [verify: any 2025+ refreshed plan], which is a natural opening to align federal and foundation strategy with new institutional priorities.

===== SECTION: Mission Statement =====
Union College provides a rigorous, holistic and immersive residential liberal education that emphasizes integration, innovation, inclusion and reflection for every student.

===== SECTION: Vision =====
Developing every student to lead with wisdom, empathy and courage, in ways large and small, now and across multiple tomorrows.

===== SECTION: Values =====
Strategic themes from The Power of Union:
- Blend the liberal arts and engineering
- Transcend disciplinary boundaries
- Bridge the classroom and immersive, experiential learning
- Engage and embrace diverse experiences and perspectives
