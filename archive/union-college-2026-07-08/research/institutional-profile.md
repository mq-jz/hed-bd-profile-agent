===== SECTION: About =====
Union College is a private, nonprofit residential liberal arts college in Schenectady, New York. Chartered in 1795, it was the first college chartered by the Board of Regents of the State of New York and is credited with laying out the first planned college campus in the United States. Union enrolls roughly 2,046 students, almost entirely undergraduates, across 21 academic departments with a 9 to 1 student-faculty ratio. Its defining feature is the integration of the liberal arts with engineering, taught here since 1845; from a funding standpoint Union is a research-active baccalaureate college with a modest, NSF-concentrated federal footprint, not a research university.
Federally, Union has drawn about $15.9 million across 57 awards since 2016, led by the National Science Foundation (about $8.98 million), with one-time Department of Education pandemic relief; excluding that relief, competitive research runs closer to $10 million per decade.
