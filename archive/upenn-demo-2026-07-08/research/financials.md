===== SECTION: Endowment and Financials =====
- Endowment: $24.8 billion FY25 (as of June 30, 2025; Penn Office of Investments). The NACUBO-Commonfund FY2025 compilation lists Penn at $24.81 billion, sixth largest among U.S. private universities. FY25 investment return was 12.2 percent, and the endowment grew about $2.5 billion year over year. Roughly $19.5 billion supports the University and $5.3 billion supports the University of Pennsylvania Health System.
- Revenue: $9.93 billion FY23 (tax year 2023 Form 990, fiscal year ended June 30, 2023, the latest filing on ProPublica)
- Expenses: $9.26 billion FY23
- Net Revenue: $660.9 million FY23
- Net Assets: $25.37 billion FY23 (end of year)
- Prior year for context: FY22 revenue $11.08 billion, expenses $8.26 billion, net revenue $2.82 billion, net assets $24.66 billion. The FY22 to FY23 revenue swing is investment-return driven, not operating decline.
- Figures disagree by scope and timing, flagged here: the 990 covers the Trustees of the University of Pennsylvania legal entity through June 2023, while Penn's audited consolidated FY2025 annual financial report (University plus Penn Medicine health system) shows total net assets of about $33.9 billion (up from about $30.95 billion in FY24) and total assets of about $44.2 billion. Both are accurate for their scope; use the 990 line items above for house-style comparability.
- BD context: this is a balance sheet without capacity constraints. Penn's financial profile signals no need for capacity-building support; any M&Q value proposition rests on targeted strategy, not financial need.
- Source: ProPublica NonProfit Explorer, Institution's IRS Form 990 (EIN 231352685); Penn Office of Investments; Penn FY2025 Annual Financial Report; NACUBO-Commonfund Study of Endowments (FY2025)

===== SECTION: HERD Ranking and Research Expenditures =====
Penn is one of the largest research enterprises in American higher education and moved up to second nationally in FY2024 total R&D expenditures, behind only Johns Hopkins, passing UC San Francisco and Michigan (NSF HERD survey, NSF 26-304). Federally financed dollars are about 47 percent of Penn's total, below the 55 percent national average, reflecting the scale of institutional funds flowing through Penn Medicine.

- 2024 All HERD Rank: 2
- 2024 All HERD Expenditures: $2,167,504,000
- 2024 Federal HERD Rank: 9 (computed from the federally financed column of NSF 26-304 Table 6; NSF's published rank column sorts by total)
- 2024 Federal HERD Expenditures: $1,018,602,000
